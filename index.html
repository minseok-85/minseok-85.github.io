About me

I am an undergraduate student at Hanyang University, Korea (B.S. Electronic Engineering, expected Feb 2027). During my exchange semester at the University of North Dakota (Jan–May 2026), I joined Prof. Jueming Hu's lab as an undergraduate researcher and have continued this research since. My current work focuses on resilient UAV navigation under sensor dropout using offline reinforcement learning. I am highly interested in pursuing a PhD starting in Fall 2027.
